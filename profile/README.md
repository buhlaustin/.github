## What I'm building

**[ClassQuip](https://classquip.com)** equips every teacher with a smarter classroom — live video, real-time participation analytics, interactive polls, instant breakout groups, and moderated chat in one high-performance platform.

Teachers shouldn't have to juggle tabs to run a lesson. ClassQuip is purpose-built for active, distraction-free digital learning:

- **Live participation analytics** — see who's engaged, who's struggling, and who needs a nudge in real time
- **Quip polls & tools** — launch polls, word clouds, and exit tickets without leaving the room
- **Instant breakout groups** — spin up rooms with one click and bring everyone back seamlessly
- **Moderated live chat** — keep conversations safe and on-topic with teacher-controlled moderation
- **Built for K-12 safety** — FERPA-aware design with role-based controls for schools and districts
- **LMS native** — deep LTI 1.3 integration with Canvas, Blackboard, Schoology, and Clever

As Chief Architect, I design and ship the platform end to end — from real-time classroom infrastructure to the teacher workflows that have to work on a Tuesday morning with 28 seventh graders.

I saw firsthand what breaks when virtual classrooms are bolted onto generic video tools instead of built for how teachers actually teach.

That experience, plus a **B.S. in Software Engineering** from Western Governors University and years of hands-on product and systems work, is what I bring to ClassQuip: education domain depth and the engineering discipline to build software that holds up in production classrooms.
